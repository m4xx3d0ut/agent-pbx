## Independent review closure

The RC1 policy review held promotion for two correctness findings:

1. a familiar managed model slug could be classified before custom-provider
   or stale-catalog provenance was considered; and
2. historical Sol elevation aliases could be persisted and later fail to
   match the canonical observed profile.

RC2 closes both findings:

- provider and catalog evidence now precede managed-profile classification;
- custom-provider, stale/empty-catalog, and unsupported live-catalog choices
  remain observational and cannot be adopted;
- adoption obtains a fresh classification rather than trusting the event-time
  projection;
- a matching elevation lease cannot override invalid provider/catalog
  provenance; and
- new and retained Sol 5.6 elevation aliases canonicalize to
  `sol-5.6-max` for request and lease matching.

The correction does not narrow Codex's native `/model` picker. It hardens only
the boundary where Agent PBX adopts a live choice into entity-local resume
metadata.
